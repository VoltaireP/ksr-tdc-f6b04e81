This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Leads counted by Meta fell to 184 from 290 the week before, and cost per lead rose to $3.01 from $1.90. Ad spend was about the same at $554.36, and landing page views fell to 353 from 393.
  • What to look into. Cost per lead has now risen two weeks in a row. The Tax Debt Book campaign spent the most, $156.58, for 50 leads at $3.13 each, while the IRS Survival Guide campaign brought 63 leads at $1.29 each on $81.24. There is no CRM feed yet.
  • What is next. We recommend moving some budget from the Tax Debt Book campaign toward the IRS Survival Guide campaign, which brought leads at less than half the cost, and holding other edits for a week so the ads launched this week can settle.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The two IRS Survival Guide image ads brought 18 leads at $1.36 and 16 at $1.32. They lead with 'The Collector doesn't stop' and 'IRS Notice in Your Mailbox? Download Your Defense Today,' and offer the free guide. The refund warning image ad brought 20 at $2.85, and the California tax board ad, 'The IRS is Scary. The FTB is Faster,' brought 20 at $2.47. We recommend testing a Collector image in the Tax Debt Book campaign, since that angle is bringing leads at about half the cost.
